# Interview extras (Three.js)

Run **before** the `presentation` interview core, except the timer (after
duration). One question at a time. Recommended default first.

## World (hard stop)

Propose 2 to 4 worlds that fit the argument. Each option is a place, not a
palette.

For each option give:

- Name
- Why this world makes the argument visible
- Hero surface in that world (screen, wall, book, hologram, chalkboard, phone)
- Two or three punch beats the camera can land (a reveal, a mechanism, a scale jump)

Recommend one. Wait. No code until the user picks or rewrites.

If the research brief is thin, still propose worlds from the topic. Do not stall
the gate to "finish research."

## Theme

After the world is locked: palette, light, mood. Tie it to brand if the user
already gave brand colors. Keep 2–3 colors. 60-30-10.

## Story

How the camera moves through that world. Recommend a path that matches the
narrative arc (hook in a wide shot, tighten into the surface for evidence, pull
back for punch, land on the takeaway).

Do not yo-yo glass → room → glass on every slide. Group punch beats per act.

## Timer (after duration)

Ask: do you want a presenter timer? Default **yes** for conference talks,
**no** for internal walkthroughs.

If yes, use the duration already given. Do not ask duration again.

- Hidden at load
- `T` shows it and starts count-up from `00:00`
- `T` again stops it and leaves the last time on screen
- Past the planned length, the time stays visible as overtime

If no, do not mount a timer. `T` does nothing.
